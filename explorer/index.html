THE MATHEMATICAL NOTE

One curve. Three distinct questions.

Value: Where is the curve? Derivative: How steep is it at one point? Integral: What is the net signed accumulation across an interval?

The tangent uses an analytic derivative for these four selected functions. The central difference and Simpson numbers are approximations, not certified error bounds. This is an educational visualization, not a general-purpose symbolic solver.
